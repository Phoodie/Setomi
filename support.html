Setomi Support

Setomi is a workout planner and logger. Your plans and history stay on your iPhone.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: dylanrmccoy@yahoo.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Back up your data

Setomi has no account or cloud copy. In the Journal tab, tap the export button and choose a backup file (JSON). Keep it somewhere safe; you can restore it from the same menu with Import workouts.

Common questions

How do I bring in my own plan? Plans tab → Import from a file, Paste notes or a table, or From a photo of your notes. Check what it found before saving.

Rest timer alerts aren't buzzing. Check Settings → Notifications → Setomi on your iPhone.

Apple Health. It's optional. Change it any time in Setomi → Settings → Health or in the Health app.
