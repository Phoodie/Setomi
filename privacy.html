Setomi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The short version. Setomi stores your workout plans and logs on your iPhone. We don't have a server, an account system or analytics, and we don't collect, receive, sell or share your data.

What Setomi keeps, and where

Your plans, workouts, weights, notes, gym choices and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device. They are not uploaded. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you exported a backup yourself.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Setomi saves your finished lifting sessions and cardio to Apple Health as workouts, reads your bodyweight to estimate walking calories, and, if you turn it on, reads strength workouts from Apple Health (date and length only) to complete your history. On Apple Watch, if you allow it, Setomi records your heart rate and calories during a lifting session (and for three minutes after it, to show how fast your heart rate recovers) and saves it as a workout. On your iPhone, if you allow it, Setomi reads that workout's heart rate and active energy, and your date of birth (to set heart-rate zones), to draw the workout's heart-rate page and exported PDF. On the Summary, if you allow it, Setomi also reads your bodyweight history, last night's sleep, your resting heart rate, heart rate variability and today's step count, to show a readiness score and trends. Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health under your control. Setomi does not send Health data anywhere, and does not use it for advertising or sell it. You can change the permission at any time in Settings → Health.

Apple Watch

The watch app and the iPhone app exchange your current workout and the sets you log over the connection Apple provides between your paired devices. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Place and weather (optional)

If you allow location access ("while using the app"), Setomi notes where each workout starts (your gym or neighbourhood) and the weather at that time, and keeps them with the workout on your iPhone, in its PDF and in backups you export. To name the place, Setomi asks Apple Maps; to get the weather, it asks Apple Weather with your location rounded to about 1 km. Both are Apple services; Setomi has no server and never receives your location. If Apple Health saving is on, the place and weather are also added to the workout in Apple Health. Turn it off any time in Setomi's Settings → Place and weather, or in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

Notifications and Live Activities (optional)

Rest-timer alerts, training-day reminders and the lock-screen timer are created on your device. No notification content leaves your phone.

Importing a plan

PDFs, spreadsheets, text and photos you choose are read on your device. Reading a photo and the optional AI step use Apple's on-device frameworks (text recognition and Apple's on-device language model). Your files are not sent to Setomi or to any third party by the app.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share a plan, a workout PDF, a journal export or a backup, you choose where it goes (Files, Messages, AirDrop and so on). That is controlled by you and by the app you share to.

Links

"Why this lift" notes may link to a published study (a DOI link). Tapping one opens it in your browser, and that site's own policy applies.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party SDKs, no profiling.

Children

Setomi is a general fitness tool and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Medical note

Setomi is a logbook and planner, not medical advice. Check with a professional before changing your training or if you have a health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
