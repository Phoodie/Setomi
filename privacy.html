Setomi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version. Setomi stores your workout plans and logs on your iPhone. We don't have a server, an account system or analytics, and we don't collect, receive, sell or share your data.

What Setomi keeps, and where

Your plans, workouts, weights, notes, gym choices and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device. They are not uploaded. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you exported a backup yourself.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Setomi saves your finished lifting sessions and cardio to Apple Health as workouts, reads your bodyweight to estimate walking calories, and, if you turn it on, reads strength workouts from Apple Health (date and length only) to complete your history. On Apple Watch, if you allow it, Setomi records your heart rate and calories during a lifting session (and for three minutes after it, to show how fast your heart rate recovers) and saves it as a workout. On your iPhone, if you allow it, Setomi reads that workout's heart rate and active energy, and your date of birth (to set heart-rate zones), to draw the workout's heart-rate page and exported PDF. On the Summary, if you allow it, Setomi also reads your bodyweight history, last night's sleep, your resting heart rate, heart rate variability and today's step count, to show a readiness score and trends. On the Heart page, if you allow it, Setomi reads your cardio fitness (VO2 max), one-minute heart rate recovery and walking heart rate average. On the Body page, if you allow it, Setomi reads body fat percentage, waist size and lean body mass that a smart scale or the Health app recorded; it never writes these. Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health under your control. Setomi does not send Health data anywhere, and does not use it for advertising or sell it. You can change the permission at any time in Settings → Health.

Body measurements and progress photos (optional)

Measurements you type and progress photos you take or choose are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. Photos are taken with your permission for the camera, or picked from your library with your permission for photos; Setomi keeps its own copy of each one you add and does not upload them anywhere. Deleting a photo in Setomi deletes that copy. Progress photos are not included in the backups Setomi exports.

Apple Watch

The watch app and the iPhone app exchange your current workout and the sets you log over the connection Apple provides between your paired devices. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Place and weather (optional)

If you allow location access ("while using the app"), Setomi notes where each workout starts (your gym or neighbourhood) and the weather at that time, and keeps them with the workout on your iPhone, in its PDF and in backups you export. To name the place, Setomi asks Apple Maps; to get the weather, it asks Apple Weather with your location rounded to about 1 km. Both are Apple services; Setomi has no server and never receives your location. If Apple Health saving is on, the place and weather are also added to the workout in Apple Health. Turn it off any time in Setomi's Settings → Place and weather, or in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

Nutrition (optional)

Food you log, your own foods and meals, your calorie, protein, carbs and fat targets, and your week plan are saved on your iPhone, like your workouts. The built-in foods (from USDA FoodData Central, and restaurant items from each chain's published nutrition) are part of the app and need no connection.

Estimates from a description or a photo of a meal, and recipes you paste, photograph or open from a file, are read on your device with Apple's text recognition and Apple's on-device model; the photo or file is not kept or sent anywhere by Setomi.

If you hold the microphone button to say what you ate, Setomi uses Apple's speech recognition with your permission for the microphone and speech recognition. On iPhones that support it, the speech is recognised on the device; on others, Apple's speech recognition may send the audio to Apple to turn it into text, under Apple's privacy policy. Setomi does not record or keep the audio.

When you scan a barcode or search for a branded food, Setomi sends only that barcode or those search words to Open Food Facts, an open food database, to get the product's nutrition. Nothing about you is sent with it. A product you log is kept among your own foods on your iPhone, so it is found next time without a connection.

If you turn on "Save food to Apple Health", the calories, protein, carbs and fat of each food you log are written to Apple Health.

Notifications and Live Activities (optional)

Rest-timer alerts, training-day reminders and the lock-screen timer are created on your device. No notification content leaves your phone.

Importing a plan

PDFs, spreadsheets, text and photos you choose are read on your device. Reading a photo and the optional AI step use Apple's on-device frameworks (text recognition and Apple's on-device language model). Your files are not sent to Setomi or to any third party by the app.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share a plan, a workout PDF, a journal export or a backup, you choose where it goes (Files, Messages, AirDrop and so on). That is controlled by you and by the app you share to.

Links

"Why this lift" notes may link to a published study (a DOI link). Tapping one opens it in your browser, and that site's own policy applies.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party SDKs, no profiling.

Children

Setomi is a general fitness tool and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Medical note

Setomi is a logbook and planner, not medical advice. Check with a professional before changing your training or if you have a health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
