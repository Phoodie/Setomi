Your plan, wherever it lives

A trainer's PDF, a spreadsheet, a note on your phone, a photo of the whiteboard. Setomi reads it on your iPhone, shows you what it found, and builds your days. Nothing is saved until you say so.

Made for the gym floor

One screen per day. Tap a set when you finish it. Last time's weight is already there, the rest timer runs on your lock screen and Dynamic Island, and the app tells you when it's time to add weight.

  • Log with the Digital Crown on Apple Watch
  • Swap any lift for a close match, or shuffle the day
  • A friendly checklist and quick tour for your first week

See yourself get stronger

Estimated one-rep maxes, top-set charts, new records and weekly streaks. A muscle map shows what you've trained, and a monthly report and year in review look back.

  • Home screen and lock screen widgets
  • Eight themes and nine app icons
